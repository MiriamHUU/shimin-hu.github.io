I'm a graduate researcher in physics working at the intersection of **photonics** and **neuroscience**. My research spans two distinct threads: the nanophotonics of **stimulated Brillouin scattering (SBS) in silicon waveguides** — device design, nanofabrication, COMSOL simulation, and theoretical formalism — and **EEG-based cognitive neuroscience**, where I design and run experiments on numerosity perception and visual feature processing.

Across both projects, my day-to-day work is really a data science practice: building simulation and analysis pipelines, designing controlled experiments, cleaning and modeling signal data (EEG time series, simulation outputs), and drawing statistically grounded conclusions from noisy, high-dimensional data. I'm now looking to bring those skills — signal processing, experimental design, statistical modeling, and scientific computing in Python/MATLAB — to an industry data science role.
