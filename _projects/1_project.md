Research on stimulated Brillouin scattering (SBS) in silicon waveguides, spanning:

- **Theoretical formalism** for the acousto-optic coupling underlying SBS in nanoscale silicon photonic structures
- **Device design and nanofabrication** of silicon waveguides engineered to enhance the SBS interaction
- **COMSOL simulation** of the coupled optical and acoustic modes to guide waveguide geometry
